## 🧭 Product case studies

**[Groww: Keep SIP Active](https://github.com/chiragpc2004/Nextleap-Groww-SIP)**: NextLeap PM Fellowship graduation project
Aims to get more first-time investors to keep their SIPs running. My research found that many SIPs stop by accident, after a late salary or a failed payment. I built **GR-1**, an AI assistant concept that steps in at that moment and shows the user every option before the SIP lapses.
- 7,600 public posts → 219 first-person stories, coded with AI and checked by hand · 6 interviews · 3 user tests
- Live MVP with an eval suite: 30/32 on the latest run, 0 unsafe answers across all 7 runs
- [MVP](https://nextleap-groww-mvp.vercel.app) · [Discovery engine](https://nextleap-groww-sip-discovery-engine.vercel.app)

**[Practice Out Loud](https://github.com/chiragpc2004/Practice-Out-Loud)**: more voice input on ChatGPT mobile in India
A case study that runs from market landscape to user research to a shipped prototype: a private voice mock interview with a transcript you can review, available in English, Hinglish or Hindi.
- Research with job seekers showed the barriers were context and control, not awareness: 65% cited people around them, and 53% wanted to edit before sending
- [Live prototype](https://practice-out-loud.vercel.app) · [3-pager](https://github.com/chiragpc2004/Practice-Out-Loud/blob/main/docs/03-solution/3-pager.pdf)
